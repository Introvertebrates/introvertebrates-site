<h2>A third <i>Hexura</i> species</h2>

<p><i>Hexura vandal</i> becomes the third named species in its genus and the first reported from the Rocky Mountains, around 500 kilometres east of the previously documented Oregon and Washington range. It also provides the genus's first taxonomic update in nearly 50 years.</p>

<h2>A forest specialist</h2>

<p>Researchers found the spider around mossy ground and decaying logs in moist conifer forest. Repeated searches found it locally abundant in suitable habitat but absent as conditions became drier. Physical differences and genetic evidence support its recognition as a distinct, long-isolated lineage.</p>

<h2>Important caveats</h2>

<p>Its full geographic range remains uncertain. Reported concerns about logging and wildfire should not be read as a formal threatened-species assessment or evidence of a measured population decline. This summary was checked against the institutional and publisher announcements; the full paper could not be retrieved during this review.</p>
