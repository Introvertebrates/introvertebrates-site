A practical review system

Evidence stays visible.

The review label tells visitors what was supplied. It does not pretend that a photograph can prove more than it actually shows.

01 · Keeper-reported

Statement received

The keeper supplied the animal, measurement, and method, but the measurement itself could not be checked from media.

02 · Photo-reviewed

Still image checked

A clear photograph shows the animal and measuring tool or tared scale. Perspective and visibility are considered during review.

03 · Video-reviewed

Continuous evidence checked

A continuous clip shows the measurement setup and result, providing the strongest practical evidence accepted by the site.

Humane measurement

Never disturb an animal for a title.

Use only calm, opportunistic measurements. If a safe view is not available, wait. No ranking is worth touching, stretching, restraining, or provoking an animal.

DLS

Diagonal leg span

  • Photograph from directly above.
  • Keep the ruler in the same plane as the animal.
  • Measure naturally extended opposite legs without contact.
WEIGHT

Tared scale

  • Tare the secure container first.
  • Show the stable reading and unit clearly.
  • Prefer a continuous clip from taring to final reading.
IDENTITY

Submitted as

  • Include the scientific name you use.
  • Share provenance only when useful and non-sensitive.
  • If identity cannot be independently confirmed, it will be marked “submitted as”.

Submit for review

Start with an email. The app can prepare the details.

For a measurement, include the scientific name, measurement and unit, method, and the clearest photo or short video available. For a spotlight, include a photograph and a few sentences about the animal or moment.

  • Say how you would like to be credited—or ask to remain anonymous.
  • Confirm that you own the submitted media and may share it.
  • Do not include exact locations, home details, or other private information.
  • Published media has identifying metadata removed where practical.

By submitting media, you give Introvertebrates non-exclusive permission to display it on the website and related Introvertebrates channels. You retain ownership and may request removal. Submission does not guarantee publication.
