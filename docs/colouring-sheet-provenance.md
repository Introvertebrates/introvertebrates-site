## Ukas dyr additions · 7 September 2026

Fifteen missing animal sheets were generated with the built-in image tool. Fine naturalistic black outlines, sparse texture, white interiors and uncluttered backgrounds follow the existing sheet style. Portrait art is fitted into the existing A4 print layout. The small existing spider logo is printed in black with a slight tilt and the line “Små kryp. Store farger.” Names and branding remain editable HTML.

The Huggorm sheet is a generic Vipera berus depiction, explicitly identified as such. The photo-based and first generic drafts were rejected by the user. The replacement uses a cleaner loose coil and outlined zigzag markings. Previously accepted conversation sheets were not available as attachments, so they were not reused. The musk-ox drafts with dark shading or an incorrect eye in the horn were rejected.
