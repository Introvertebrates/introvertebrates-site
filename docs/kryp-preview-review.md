# Kryp & Krabater redesign

Approved for publication by the user on 8 September 2026 after review of the second local design.

The original landscape hero and typography are retained in a shorter homepage. Five navigation links lead to animals, weekly features, films, colouring and about. The animal directory separates Norwegian wildlife from the personal collection, provides search and shows one selected profile with working direct links and a no-JavaScript fallback.

Lappugle and sidensvans now include identification, habitat and feeding information with Cornell Lab sources. Existing local videos remain below the profiles.

Weekly order, approved media decisions and scheduled colouring-sheet release rules are unchanged. New public pages include descriptions, canonical URLs and social metadata.
