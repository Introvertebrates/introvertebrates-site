From encounter to understanding

Let the animal lead the story.

A tongue flick, a sensory hair, a track in snow, or a bird returning to its nest can begin with a simple question. The aim is to answer clearly, show the real animal, and leave room for the next question.

Made for young viewers

Curiosity before fear.

The main information here is in English so more people can explore it. The films themselves are rooted in Norwegian language, landscapes, and a child-friendly way of telling animal stories.

01 · Real animals

Authentic encounters

Personal footage and photography keep the animals at the centre—whether they live in the collection or were met in the field.

02 · Clear explanations

Accurate, never sensational

Venom, predators, unfamiliar body plans, and animal behaviour are explained without turning them into scares.

03 · Open questions

A reason to look again

Each story should end with more attention to the garden, shoreline, forest, mountains, or animals close to home.
