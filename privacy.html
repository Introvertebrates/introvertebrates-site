Privacy

Analytics is optional here.

Introvertebrates uses analytics to understand which pages people find, where visits come from, and how the site is used. Google Analytics is not loaded unless a visitor explicitly allows analytics.

What Google Analytics can measure

When analytics is allowed, the site can record information such as pages viewed, approximate region, device and browser type, referral source, scrolls, outbound link clicks, and other engagement events provided by Google Analytics 4 Enhanced Measurement.

What is disabled

Introvertebrates disables Google advertising signals and ad-personalisation signals in its analytics configuration. Analytics is used to improve the site and understand its audience, not to build advertising profiles.

Your choice

If you choose “No thanks”, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded. If you allow analytics, your choice is remembered in your browser for up to 180 days. You can change the choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer of pages where optional analytics is available.

Kryp & Krabater

The child-focused Kryp & Krabater section is intentionally excluded from Google Analytics tracking.
